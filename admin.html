Events Configuration

Events are managed in the Google Sheet "Events" tab. Changes are live immediately.

How it works

7 Events: Lg (Lagnotri), MS (Mehendi & Sangeet), Ma (Mandvo), Lu (Luncheon), MG (Meet & Greet), We (Wedding), BT (Black Tie).

Luncheon is for the bride’s side, on the same day as the Mandvo. Guests invited to the Mandvo don’t get a separate Luncheon card — it’s part of their Mandvo. Give Luncheon seats only to lunch-only guests (not invited to the Mandvo).

Invitation codes are unique 6-character codes generated automatically for each guest (never reuse or guess them).

Seats per event are set on each guest — a guest can only RSVP up to their allocation.

Full step-by-step workflows are on the 📖 Help tab — or tap Teach me at the top.

📖 Teach me how to use this

Everything an admin needs, in the order you'll actually do it. Tap a section to open it. Buttons are written like this so you can match them on screen.

The three things you'll do most: 1 · Upload the guest list → 2 · Send invites → 3 · Watch the responses come in.
Walks you through the real screens. Nothing gets changed. Press Esc to leave at any point.
0 · Getting in & staying safe
  • Everyone uses the same admin PIN. Don't paste it into group chats — send it to each person directly.
  • On your own phone, leave Keep me signed in on this phone ticked when you enter the PIN — you won’t be asked again for 14 days, even after a refresh.
  • On a shared or family phone, untick it, and tap Logout when you're done. Without it ticked, the session ends by itself after 30 minutes of no activity.
  • Each person's filters and view are remembered on that phone/tab only. Two people on different phones never see each other's filters.
1 · Upload the guest list

Best for adding many guests at once. Go to the 📤 CSV Upload tab.

  1. Tap ⬇ Download Template. Open it in Excel / Google Sheets / Numbers.
  2. One row per invitation (a couple who share an invite = one row). Fill in:
    • first_name — the name exactly as you want it on the invite. "Paul & Mehreen" is fine here. This is what the guest will type to sign in.
    • last_name — optional. Leave blank if the name above is complete.
    • phone — include the country code, e.g. +254712345678. Needed for WhatsApp.
    • email — optional but lets us send a confirmation.
    • relationship — one of: Bride's Family, Groom's Family, Bride's Friend, Groom's Friend. (Or leave it and use the dropdown in step 4.)
    • group — optional sub-group within the relationship, e.g. Jays Friends, Business, London. Lets you filter the guest list and send invites one group at a time.
    • is_overseas — TRUE or FALSE.
    • One … Guests column per event. Put either a number — how many seats; the family decides who comes, and the guest page says “Name and Family” when it’s more than the people on the invite — or the exact names, comma-separated (e.g. Black Tie: Mike, Tinu, Krisha), when only those people are invited. The guest ticks who’s coming from that list and can’t add anyone else. 0 or blank = not invited to that event.
    • Black Tie Table — optional table number. Only the Black Tie has a table plan.
    • invitation_code — leave blank. A unique code is created for each row automatically.
  3. Save as CSV, then tap the box that says Click to select a CSV file (or drag the file onto it). You'll see a preview of every row.
  4. If every row in this file is the same relationship, pick it under Apply relationship to every row — it saves typing it 200 times.
  5. Tap Upload Guests. Any row with a problem is listed with its row number; fix those in the file and upload just those rows again.

Already have your own master sheet? Save it as CSV and upload it as-is. Columns like Cover Name, Phone No, and one column per event (Lagnotri, Mendhi, Mandvo, Meet & Greet, Wedding, Blacktie) are recognised. In an event column, a number means seats and names mean only those people. Check the preview before tapping Upload.

Adding one guest? On the 👥 Guests tab tap + Add Guest. Tick the events they're invited to and set seats per event. For an event where only certain people are invited, type their names in the box under that event instead. The code is generated for you.

On the guest's RSVP page each event shows who it's for: "First & Last and Family" when that event has more than a couple's worth of seats (more than 2, or more than the people named), just "First & Last" when it doesn't, or the list of reserved names. So Shan Shah with 2 seats shows just the name; with 5 seats it shows "Shan Shah and Family".
The Needs attention strip at the top of the Guests tab tells you how many guests are missing a phone, email or relationship — tap a chip to see just those and fix them.
2 · Send out the invites

Go to the ✉ Messages tab. Invites go out one at a time over WhatsApp from your phone, so guests see it come from you.

  1. Before your first send, go to ⚙️ Settings → Message Templates and tap 💬 Send test to my phone. Read it on WhatsApp and fix any typo once there rather than in 700 messages.
  2. On Messages, make sure Invites is selected at the top, then tap your group — e.g. Groom’s Family. Each card shows how many invites are left.
  3. You now only see that group. Tap ▶ Start sending.
  4. Each guest gets their own screen with their events and the exact message.
    • Tap 💬 Open WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens with the message ready — press send there, then come back.
    • Tap Next: <name> →. The guest is marked as sent automatically.
    • Want a personal line? Tap ✎ Edit this message first. It only changes this one message.
    • Not now? Skip for now. They wait under the Skipped tab.

Handing a group to family: open the group and tap Share this list. Opening that link lands them straight on that group.

Guests without a phone number are under the No phone tab. Tap Add phone, or ✉ Email if we have their email.

Chasing people who haven’t replied: switch to Reminders at the top. Same flow, a reminder message, and only guests who haven’t responded. Opening WhatsApp records the reminder, so Next moves on and they appear under Reminded (on every phone). Anyone still not replied after 7 days comes back into the queue for another nudge. WhatsApp didn’t send? Tap Put back in the queue.

Sending everyone their event details: switch to Event details. Only guests who’ve replied yes appear. Each message lists their events (via {{events}}): date & time, their side’s venue, dress code, extra details and how many are coming. Edit the wording in Settings → Message Templates (“Event details”). Sent guests move to Sent on every phone.

WhatsApp didn’t actually send? Tap Mark as not sent under the Next button and they go back in the queue. Already-sent guests are under the Sent tab — tap one to resend.
3 · Review the responses

Quick glance — 👥 Guests tab:

  • The cards at the top: total guests, how many have RSVP'd, how many are still pending, and confirmed headcount.
  • Recent RSVPs shows the last few people who responded and how long ago.
  • Tap ✓ RSVP Status to see two columns: everyone who's submitted, everyone who hasn't.
  • Coloured dots on every row: ● invite not sent   ● sent, awaiting reply   ● replied.

The detail — 📋 RSVPs tab:

  • Everything is counted in people, not invites. A couple who said yes counts as 2.
  • Overview — invites replied, people coming, invites not replied (and how many invites aren't sent yet), people who can't come at all.
  • Headcount by event — for every event: Coming, Declined, Waiting (no reply yet) and Invited. Tap any number to see exactly who.
  • Possible final numbers — per event (and per side): Confirmed, Still could (invited but not replied) and Up to = the most that could come if everyone still to reply says yes.
  • Breakdown — pick an event and split it by relationship, group, or overseas / in Kenya. Tap any number to see who.
  • The list at the bottom shows whatever you tapped (or the latest replies). Tap a name to open the guest — from there you can record or change their reply. Copy names and Export list give you that exact list; All replies downloads every reply.
  • The dropdowns at the top (relationship, group, overseas, search) narrow every number on the page.

Seating: Guests tab → 📅 By Event → pick the event. For anyone attending you can type a table number straight into the row. Export Seating CSV gives the venue a list.

If the RSVP Status view shows a ⚠ Duplicate Submissions box, someone responded twice. Only the newest counts. Tap Delete this submission on the older one to tidy it up.
Hotel & travel (overseas guests)
  • The 🏨 Travel tab lists every overseas guest (and anyone else you've recorded travel for).
  • Tap a guest to record what they told you: do they need the Glam Hotel, rooms, people staying, check-in/out if not the covered 25–30 December (any nights outside that are shown as guest pays), arrival and departure flights, and airport pickup / drop-off.
  • The cards at the top count invites and people: need the hotel (with total rooms), don't need it, not answered yet, airport pickups and drop-offs, and hotel guests with no flight yet. Tap a card to list those guests.
  • Arrivals & departures (second tab) lists arrivals and departures side by side, by day and time, for planning drivers.
  • In a guest's form, tick Match hotel dates to flights to check in on the arrival date and check out on the departure date; it keeps up as you change the flights, and shows the nights we cover and any the guest pays for.
  • ↓ Hotel list is ready to send to the hotel; ↓ Airport transfers to the driver.
  • For a guest who isn't marked overseas, open them on the Guests tab (👁 View) and tap 🏨 Hotel & travel.
4 · Fixing things
  • Edit a guest — Guests tab, tap ✎ Edit on their row. Change anything, tap Save Guest.
  • "Updated by someone else" message when saving? Another admin edited the same guest while you had it open. Tap Reload, then make your change again. Nothing was lost.
  • Delete a guest — tap ✕ Delete, tick Yes, I'm sure and confirm. You get Undo for 8 seconds, and they can always be restored from the Deleted section at the bottom of the list.
  • Change lots of guests at once — tick the boxes on the left of the rows (or the top box for the whole page). A gold bar appears with: mark invite sent, set relationship, tag overseas, delete selected.
  • A guest RSVP'd wrong, or replied by phone — open the guest (Guests tab 👁 View, or tap their name in the RSVPs tab), then ✎ Change reply or ✎ Record their reply. Guests can't change their own answer once sent; they contact you.
  • A guest wants to start their RSVP again (or you're clearing a test) — Guests tab, tap 👁 View on their row, then ↺ Reset RSVP. Their answers are cleared and they go back to Awaiting RSVP, so they can sign in and respond again. Their invite and code stay the same.
  • A guest says their code doesn't work — check the name on their row matches what they're typing. They must type the name exactly as it appears on the invite (spaces and capitals don't matter, but "Paul & Mehreen" won't match "Paul").
  • Can't find someone — check the filters at the top of the Guests tab; tap Clear filters.
5 · Settings (do once)
  • Message Settings — the RSVP website address, the reply-by dates, and your default country code (e.g. +254) so phone numbers without one still work. There's a Copy button next to the website address.
  • Message Templates — the actual text of the invite and chase-up. Use the buttons to drop in {{name}}, {{code}}, {{url}} and {{deadline}} — they're replaced for each guest. Open Preview with sample guest to see the result. Always Send test to my phone before a real send.
  • Relationship Defaults — which template each group gets by default (family might get a more formal one than friends). You can still change it per guest in the preview.
  • Events Configuration is read-only here — events are edited in the Google Sheet's Events tab.
6 · If something looks wrong
  • "The site is busy right now" — wait a minute and try again. It protects the site from being flooded.
  • "We're updating the guest list" (a guest sees this) — someone is uploading a big CSV. It clears in under a minute.
  • A red banner about legacy event IDs on load — tell whoever set the site up; it's a one-off fix in the Google Sheet script.
  • The dashboard feels stale — pull down to refresh, or tap the tab again. Everything re-fetches.
  • Still stuck — screenshot what you see and send it to whoever set the site up.
